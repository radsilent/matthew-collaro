Matthew Collaro

Systems engineer. Founder and Platform Systems Architect at Vector Stream Systems LLC, and Senior Systems Engineer at Lucid Motors.

I have spent seven years modeling, integrating, and verifying systems in environments where unverified artifacts do not ship — commercial aircraft networks, strategic missile subsystems, naval concept architectures, and production electric vehicles. That work has run through Boeing Commercial Airplanes, Northrop Grumman Space Systems, Raytheon Missiles & Defense, Deloitte Government & Public Services, and Parker Hannifin Aerospace.

These days I split my time between vehicle systems integration and building the tooling I kept needing and never found: a governed engineering platform that keeps requirements, architecture models, and verification evidence linked to each other instead of drifting apart.

What I work on

Model-based systems engineering. SysML behavioral and interface modeling, requirements traceability, DoDAF and UAF viewpoints, and the unglamorous governance work that decides whether a model is an authoritative artifact or an expensive drawing.

Guardrails for generated engineering artifacts. Most of my career has been in places where you cannot merge something because it looks right. I apply the same posture to LLM output: generate, then check against constraints the generator cannot route around. Schema enforcement and constraint validation, not after-the-fact review.

Toolchain integration. Getting CATIA, Cameo, DOORS, JAMA, MATLAB, and Ansys to agree on what the system actually is, and exposing that context to automated tooling through controlled interfaces rather than open-ended access.
